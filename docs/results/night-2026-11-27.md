# The night of 26–27 November: the registered natural-product result

**COCONUT and PubChem are not told apart on the origin benchmark, at either
corpus size, at any label budget, on the fine-tuning path.** The differences are
no larger than what redrawing PubChem produces. This is the registered
comparison of Amendment 19(f), and it is a bounded null rather than a failure to
run.

1,320 fine-tuning cells: 11 arms × 3 origin tasks × 10 frozen scaffold splits ×
4 label budgets. Every job COMPLETED. Scored with `scripts/evaluate.py`; the
contrasts below are paired on the split, with the three origin tasks averaged
inside a split first, because they share molecules and splits and are one
benchmark read three times.

A dated note, not an amendment. Nothing here changes a registered recipe.

## 1. The noise floor

`pubchem_n25000_s0` minus `pubchem_n25000_s1`: two draws of one corpus, one
size, one recipe. No corpus effect is in this difference, so its spread is the
null distribution of every corpus contrast in the design.

| Labels | Fine-tuning | Linear probe |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | −1.01 ±1.48 | +0.42 ±1.38 |
| 500 | +0.14 ±0.60 | +0.66 ±0.86 |
| 1,000 | +0.04 ±0.92 | +0.43 ±0.65 |
| 5,000 | −0.39 ±0.65 | not run |

**Against a gate of about 1.0 AUROC point the benchmark clears at 500, 1,000 and
5,000 labels and fails at 100.** The low-label regime is the one the
natural-product hypothesis is about, and it is the one this benchmark cannot
resolve. A single redraw of the same corpus moves a 100-label cell by a point,
with an interval half as wide again.

The probe floor is in `docs/results/noise-floor/README.md`; both are read the
same way and agree from 500 labels up.

## 2. The fungal slice

The slice singled out as the registered-path test, fungal alone, ten splits.
AUROC points.

| Contrast | 100 | 500 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Noise floor**, PubChem 25k redraw | +1.27 ±3.09 | +0.12 ±0.69 | −0.91 ±2.67 | −0.45 ±1.51 |
| **COCONUT 25k − PubChem 25k** | +1.79 ±6.80 | −0.08 ±1.02 | −1.33 ±2.67 | −0.97 ±1.46 |
| **COCONUT 5k − PubChem 5k** | +1.10 ±2.29 | −2.05 ±2.73 | +0.56 ±1.61 | −0.58 ±0.67 |
| PubChem 25k − no pretraining | +1.22 ±5.37 | +5.23 ±2.18 | +2.47 ±2.30 | +1.80 ±1.22 |
| COCONUT 25k − no pretraining | +3.01 ±5.55 | +5.16 ±2.69 | +1.14 ±2.14 | +0.83 ±1.63 |
| Chemprop + Mordred − no pretraining | +3.47 ±4.68 | +2.63 ±2.81 | +1.44 ±2.24 | −1.26 ±2.14 |
| Morgan forest − no pretraining | +0.22 ±4.64 | +1.80 ±2.71 | +1.42 ±2.21 | +1.38 ±1.27 |

Every COCONUT-minus-PubChem interval contains zero, and every one of them
contains the noise floor's own estimate. Pooled over the three tasks the same
holds: −1.52 ±5.19, +0.02 ±0.64, −0.34 ±0.85, −0.10 ±0.48.

**The probe's hint does not survive.** The linear probe put COCONUT +1.16 ±0.59
ahead at 1,000 labels pooled; fine-tuned it is −0.34 ±0.85. 19(f) said a probe
screens and never concludes, and this is that rule earning its place.

**The budget interaction reverses too.** The probe's difference grew with the
budget, against the hypothesis. On the fine-tuning path there is no trend at
all, in either direction.

### What pretraining does buy

From 500 labels up the 25,000-molecule encoders beat Chemprop + Mordred by 1.4
to 3.1 points pooled, intervals excluding zero. **On this benchmark the encoders
are not merely a lossy copy of the descriptors they were trained to predict**,
which is the opposite of the ESOL finding and the first evidence in the project
against the descriptor-confound reading.

At 100 labels nothing is separable from anything: the Morgan forest is the best
pooled arm (+3.56 ±3.45), and plain Chemprop is the worst (−5.75 ±3.40).

## 3. Exclusion losses per arm

**The asymmetry is unfixed and is the main limitation of this result.**

| Arm | Benchmark molecules removed | Kept in the draw |
|---|---:|---:|
| `coconut_noleakskel_*` (all 5 draws) | 129,107 of 670,351 cleaned, **19.26%** | 0 |
| `pubchem_n25000_s{0,1}` | none, never excluded against this benchmark | about 1.5% of the draw |
| `pubchem_n5000_s{0,1}` | none | about 1.5% of the draw |

The PubChem draws record an exclusion against BBBP and ESOL (2,999 molecules,
2,393 in the pool), not against the origin benchmark. Their origin overlap is
17.2% of the benchmark across the whole PubChem cache.

Making this symmetric means drawing benchmark-excluded PubChem corpora and
pretraining them from scratch, about 8 GPU-hours each. It was not done tonight
because it would have displaced the COCONUT seeds. **Since COCONUT did not win,
the asymmetry does not explain the result**: the arm that kept its exposure is
the one that failed to pull ahead.

## 4. The three COCONUT seeds

Pretraining `coconut_noleakskel_n25000_s1`, `_n25000_s2` and `_n5000_s1`, so the
corpus draw finally has an error bar. **Running at the time of writing, started
15:46 on 27 November**, about 8 hours each at the measured rate.

They did not run overnight as planned. The prep job (30685487) drew and
described all three corpora correctly by 02:26, then **did not exit** and was
killed at its 4-hour limit at 05:52. `afterok` therefore cancelled all three
pretrainings before they started. The corpora were already on disk and verified,
so resubmitting without the dependency was enough: 30737513, 30737514, 30737516.

The hang is unexplained. Every logged step completed and `== done` was printed.
Worth a look before that script is reused.

Other faults handled: the serial Mordred loop in `finetune.py` would have
repeated the 3-hour timeout, so the task descriptor cache was seeded from the
parallel store built on 26 November, after checking the 60,170 molecules match
the task source row for row. A watcher mistook a Slurm controller outage for an
empty queue and reported "queue drained" while 23 jobs were still live;
`sacct` keeps working during such an outage and `squeue` does not.

One earlier reporting error, corrected here: half-widths quoted from the first
probe pass treated the nine task-by-split cells as independent. The three tasks
share one set of splits, so the split is the unit and those intervals were too
narrow. Everything in this note collapses tasks inside a split first.

## 6. What this leaves

- **The registered natural-product question is answered**, as a bound: at 25,000
  molecules, on this benchmark, a COCONUT-versus-PubChem effect larger than
  about 0.5 to 1 AUROC point is excluded from 500 labels up. At 100 labels the
  benchmark cannot answer and says so.
- **Still open:** the corpus-draw error bar (tonight), symmetric exclusion
  (unbudgeted), Amendment 18's tables, and Léonard's signature on 16 to 19.
- **Reproduce with:** `scripts/np_origin_paired.py` and
  `scripts/np_origin_noise_floor.py` over `runs/finetune_a19/results_per_run.csv`;
  figures from `scripts/plot_np_origin_finetune.py`.
